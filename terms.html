Terms of use

These terms describe the public Nexora pilot. By using Nexora, you agree to use it responsibly and understand the limits below.

Educational use

Nexora provides study-planning tools and links to learning and opportunity resources. It is for general educational support, not professional, legal, financial, admissions, or scholarship advice. Rankings, eligibility, deadlines, offers, and AI answers may be incomplete or outdated. Verify important details with the official source before acting.

Accounts and your information

Keep your sign-in credentials private and use an email address you control. You are responsible for information you enter into your account, NEXA chats, study rooms, and mentor forms. Do not submit passwords, financial details, health data, government identifiers, or private school records.

If you sign in, supported workspace data is synced to your account. Use the account panel’s “Delete account & cloud data” action to permanently delete your account and linked Nexora records. Nexora clears its local workspace from the browser used for the deletion; clear Nexora browser data on your other devices too.

AI and third-party services

NEXA is optional and is available only when its provider connection is configured. When enabled, prompts are sent to Google Gemini. AI output can be wrong and is not a substitute for official information. External learning, government, scholarship, and event websites are operated under their own terms and privacy practices.

Study rooms and mentoring

Room messages are visible to other participants. Mentor profiles may show an applicant’s email only when they have chosen public contact and an administrator approves the profile. Be respectful, do not post sensitive information, and report unsafe or abusive behavior.

Acceptable use and availability

Do not use Nexora to harass others, spam, break laws, interfere with the service, or access another person’s data. Nexora is an early service that may change, be unavailable, or contain errors. The service does not promise uninterrupted access or particular educational, admissions, or scholarship results.

Support and account deletion

For bugs, accessibility issues, or abuse reports, use the Nexora support issue tracker ↗. It is public: never include account emails, recovery links, private messages, or other personal data. Account and cloud-data deletion is available from the signed-in account panel.

Read the Privacy, data and support notice for what Nexora stores and its retention periods.

Last updated: November 1, 2026. These pilot terms may be updated as Nexora changes.
